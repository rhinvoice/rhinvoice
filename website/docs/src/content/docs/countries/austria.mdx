An Austrian invoice from Rhinvoice carries **20 %, 10 % or 13 % VAT** (§ 10 UStG 1994) on the date of supply, your **UID** above EUR 400 (§ 11 UStG), above EUR 10,000 also your business customer's UID, the legal notice for reverse charge, small businesses or tax-free supplies, and a **GiroCode** (EPC QR code). With the interface language *Deutsch (Österreich)* Rhinvoice writes months the Austrian way, with "Jänner"; documents print dates as numbers, such as 05.01.2026.

<Aside type="note" title="No tax advice">
As of October 2026. Rates and rules are checked against the UStG 1994 and the Austrian Economic Chamber (WKO), but without warranty. Ask your tax adviser when in doubt.
</Aside>

## Which VAT rate applies?

| Rate | In Rhinvoice |
|---|---|
| 20 % | standard rate |
| 10 % | reduced rate |
| 13 % | second reduced rate |

Source: § 10 UStG 1994. Rhinvoice holds these rates for supplies from 1 January 2016. The rate on the **date of supply** applies: the end of the service period, else its start, else the invoice date. When issuing, Rhinvoice freezes the rate of every line.

## Required details under § 11 UStG

Before issuing, Rhinvoice checks the amounts from which a UID is needed:

| Invoice total, gross | What Rhinvoice asks for |
|---|---|
| up to EUR 400 | small-amount invoice (§ 11 Abs. 6 UStG): no UID needed |
| above EUR 400 | your **UID** (§ 11 Abs. 1 Z 3 lit. i UStG) |
| above EUR 10,000 to a business | also the **customer's UID** (§ 11 Abs. 1 Z 3 lit. b UStG) |

Enter your UID under *Settings → Company* in the field *VAT number (UID)*, in the form `ATU12345678`. The customer's UID belongs to the customer; Rhinvoice asks for it above EUR 10,000 for business customers in Austria. Amounts in currencies other than EUR never count as small amounts.

Without a service period, Rhinvoice prints the invoice date as the date of supply (§ 11 Abs. 1 Z 3 lit. d UStG). It assigns the invoice number only when issuing, without gaps. Names and addresses of both sides, the lines and the net amount and VAT per rate complete the invoice.

## Small business

Switch on *Small business (§ 6 Abs. 1 Z 27 UStG)* under *Settings → Company*. Your invoices then contain no VAT and this notice:

> Umsatzsteuerfrei aufgrund der Kleinunternehmerregelung (§ 6 Abs. 1 Z 27 UStG).

(English documents: "No VAT is charged (small business scheme)." French and Italian ones name the small business exemption in the words of the EU VAT Directive, such as "Esente da IVA in virtù della franchigia per le piccole imprese (§ 6 Abs. 1 Z 27 UStG).") Rhinvoice asks small businesses for no UID. Change the wording under *Settings → Texts → Legal notices*.

## Reverse charge and supplies abroad

Rhinvoice decides the tax treatment from your country, the customer's country, whether the customer is a business and whether it has a UID. Goods and services are treated separately, on the same invoice too.

| Case | VAT | Notice on German documents |
|---|---|---|
| Customer in Austria | yes | none |
| Service to a business in another EU country with a UID | no | "Übergang der Steuerschuld auf den Leistungsempfänger (Reverse Charge)." (§ 11 Abs. 1a UStG) |
| Goods to a business in another EU country with a UID | no | "Steuerfreie innergemeinschaftliche Lieferung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 i. V. m. Art. 7 UStG)." |
| Goods leaving the EU, private customers included | no | "Steuerfreie Ausfuhrlieferung (§ 6 Abs. 1 Z 1 i. V. m. § 7 UStG)." |
| Service to a business outside the EU | no | "Nicht im Inland steuerbare Leistung." |
| Private customer inside or outside the EU (service) | yes | none |

Without the customer's UID there is no reverse charge: an EU business without a UID is charged Austrian VAT, and the editor points this out. For intra-Community supplies Rhinvoice needs your UID and the customer's, and reminds you of the proof that the goods arrived. You can change the tax treatment by hand in the editor; Rhinvoice logs the change with a reason in the history.

Distance sales to private customers in the EU keep Austrian VAT. Rhinvoice does not track the EU-wide threshold of EUR 10,000 a year (OSS).

## GiroCode (EPC QR code)

Every invoice in **EUR** gets a **GiroCode** next to the payment terms, following the European Payments Council guidelines (EPC069-12), with your IBAN, the account holder, the amount and the invoice number as remittance text. Your customer scans it with a banking app. You need an account in EUR under *Settings → Bank accounts*.

## E-invoice

Rhinvoice embeds a **ZUGFeRD / Factur-X invoice** (EN 16931) in the PDF of every issued invoice by default (switch: *Settings → Print template*), as long as the details it needs are complete; accounting software reads it directly. The Austrian format ebInterface for the public sector is not supported. More under [e-invoice](/docs/invoices/e-invoice/).

## Keeping invoices

Keep invoices for **seven years** from the end of the calendar year (§ 132 Abs. 1 BAO). From issuing on, content and PDF are locked in Rhinvoice, in the database too, and the PDF is kept with its SHA-256. Corrections are made only by a [cancellation invoice](/docs/invoices/issue-and-cancel/) with its own number. Protect the data with a [backup](/docs/operations/backup/). Try everything in the demo at [at.rhinvoice.com](https://at.rhinvoice.com).

Your company's country is missing? Rhinvoice can be extended: [Adding more countries](/docs/countries/more-countries/).
