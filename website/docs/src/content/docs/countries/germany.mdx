A German invoice from Rhinvoice carries **19 % or 7 % VAT** (§ 12 UStG) on the date of supply, your **tax number or VAT ID** (§ 14 Abs. 4 Nr. 2 UStG), the date of supply, the legal notice for reverse charge, small businesses or tax-free supplies, a **GiroCode** (EPC QR code) and an embedded **e-invoice** (ZUGFeRD). For public authorities Rhinvoice also writes the **XRechnung**.

<Aside type="note" title="No tax advice">
As of October 2026. Rates and rules are checked against the UStG and UStDV, but without warranty. Ask your tax adviser when in doubt.
</Aside>

## Which VAT rate applies?

| Valid | Standard rate | Reduced rate |
|---|---|---|
| since 1 January 2021 | 19 % | 7 % |
| 1 July 2020 to 31 December 2020 | 16 % | 5 % |
| 1 January 2007 to 30 June 2020 | 19 % | 7 % |

Source: § 12 UStG; the 2020 rates come from the Zweites Corona-Steuerhilfegesetz.

The rate on the **date of supply** applies: the end of the service period, else its start, else the invoice date. When issuing, Rhinvoice freezes the rate of every line.

## Required details under § 14 UStG

Rhinvoice checks before issuing what it can check and prints the rest from your data:

- **Tax number or VAT ID** of your company (§ 14 Abs. 4 Nr. 2 UStG). Enter them under *Settings → Company*. Without either, Rhinvoice does not issue the invoice. The exception: **small-amount invoices up to EUR 250 gross** (§ 33 UStDV). The VAT ID has the form `DE123456789`.
- **Date of supply** (§ 14 Abs. 4 Nr. 6 UStG). Without a service period, Rhinvoice prints the invoice date as the date of supply.
- **Sequential invoice number.** Rhinvoice assigns it only when issuing, without gaps or duplicates.
- **Net amount and VAT per rate**, your and the customer's name and address, date and lines.

Small businesses state their tax number or VAT ID too (§ 34a UStDV).

## Small business under § 19 UStG

Switch on *Small business (§ 19 UStG)* under *Settings → Company*. Your invoices then contain no VAT and this notice:

> Gemäß § 19 UStG wird keine Umsatzsteuer berechnet (Kleinunternehmerregelung).

(English documents: "No VAT is charged (small business scheme)." French and Italian ones name the small business exemption in the words of the EU VAT Directive: "Aucune TVA n’est facturée en vertu du § 19 UStG (franchise pour les petites entreprises)." and "Nessuna IVA addebitata ai sensi del § 19 UStG (franchigia per le piccole imprese).") Since 2025 the invoice states that § 19 UStG applies; this text does. You can change it under *Settings → Texts → Legal notices*.

## Reverse charge and supplies abroad

Rhinvoice decides the tax treatment from your country, the customer's country, whether the customer is a business and whether it has a VAT ID. Goods and services are treated separately, on the same invoice too.

| Case | VAT | Notice on German documents |
|---|---|---|
| Customer in Germany | yes | none |
| Service to a business in another EU country with a VAT ID | no | "Steuerschuldnerschaft des Leistungsempfängers." (§ 14a Abs. 5 UStG) |
| Goods to a business in another EU country with a VAT ID | no | "Steuerfreie innergemeinschaftliche Lieferung (§ 4 Nr. 1 Buchst. b i. V. m. § 6a UStG)." |
| Goods leaving the EU, private customers included | no | "Steuerfreie Ausfuhrlieferung (§ 4 Nr. 1 Buchst. a i. V. m. § 6 UStG)." |
| Service to a business outside the EU | no | "Nicht im Inland steuerbare Leistung." |
| Private customer inside or outside the EU (service) | yes | none |

Good to know:

- **No reverse charge without the customer's VAT ID.** An EU business without a VAT ID is charged German VAT. The editor points this out.
- **Intra-Community supply**: the invoice states your VAT ID and the customer's (§ 14a Abs. 3 UStG). Rhinvoice does not issue it without both, and reminds you of the proof of arrival (Gelangensbestätigung or proof of dispatch) and the EC Sales List (Zusammenfassende Meldung).
- **Domestic reverse charge under § 13b UStG** (for example § 13b Abs. 2 Nr. 7 UStG) is chosen by hand in the editor. Rhinvoice logs every change of the tax treatment with a reason in the history.
- **Distance sales to private customers in the EU** keep German VAT. Rhinvoice does not track the EU-wide threshold of EUR 10,000 a year (OSS).

## GiroCode (EPC QR code)

Every invoice in **EUR** gets a **GiroCode** next to the payment terms, following the European Payments Council guidelines (EPC069-12). It holds your IBAN, the account holder, the amount and "Rechnung" with the invoice number as remittance text; your customer scans it with a banking app. You need an account in EUR under *Settings → Bank accounts*. Invoices in other currencies have no GiroCode, drafts a placeholder.

## E-invoice: ZUGFeRD and XRechnung

Since 2025, businesses in Germany must be able to **receive** e-invoices (§ 14 UStG). They must **issue** them for domestic B2B invoices in stages (§ 27 Abs. 38 UStG):

| From | Who |
|---|---|
| 2027 | businesses with a turnover above EUR 800,000 in the previous year |
| 2028 | all businesses |

Small businesses are exempt (§ 34a UStDV).

Rhinvoice embeds a **ZUGFeRD / Factur-X invoice** (profile EN 16931) in the PDF of every issued invoice by default (switch: *Settings → Print template*). If a detail it needs is missing, the PDF is created without it. The PDF looks the same to your customers; their accounting software reads the data directly. Rhinvoice shows on the invoice whether the e-invoice is embedded. For public authorities you also download the **XRechnung** (XML), with the Leitweg-ID that you enter once on the customer (*Leitweg-ID or fixed buyer reference*), or else the invoice's *Reference*. Details and what it needs: [e-invoice](/docs/invoices/e-invoice/).

## GoBD and keeping invoices

Keep invoices for **ten years** (§ 147 AO). Rhinvoice makes them immutable:

- From issuing on, content and PDF are locked, directly in the database too.
- The PDF is kept with its SHA-256 hash.
- Corrections are made only by a [cancellation invoice](/docs/invoices/issue-and-cancel/) with its own number. Drafts can be deleted, issued invoices never.
- Every change (issued, payment, cancellation, tax treatment) is in the history with a timestamp.

Protect the data with a [backup](/docs/operations/backup/). Try everything in the demo at [de.rhinvoice.com](https://de.rhinvoice.com).

Your company's country is missing? Rhinvoice can be extended: [Adding more countries](/docs/countries/more-countries/).
