Rhinvoice writes **quotes, invoices, cancellation invoices and payment reminders** for businesses in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Germany and Austria, with each country's VAT rates, legal notices, payment QR codes and e-invoice formats. This page lists every feature of the current version as a short list. Each group links to the detailed guide.

## Quotes and invoices

- Quotes, invoices and cancellation invoices in CHF or EUR
- Editor with the form on the left and a live paper preview on the right
- Drafts save themselves; drafts can be deleted
- Lines with quantity, unit, price, discount and VAT rate, plus text lines for headings and notes
- Reorder lines by dragging or with Alt+↑ / Alt+↓
- Articles are suggested while you type in the line; *Save as article* keeps a new line as an article
- Line discount and document discount as a percentage or an amount, net or gross prices
- Subject, reference, service period, due date and payment terms per document
- Create a new customer right from the invoice, in a panel beside it
- What issuing still needs is listed on top of the editor, with a button that opens the form for it
- Numbers are assigned only when issuing, without gaps and without duplicates
- Number sequences with your own pattern (`{YYYY}`, `{YY}`, `{MM}`, `{NNNN}`), yearly reset and a preview of the next number
- Issued documents are locked, also in the database, and the PDF is kept with its SHA-256 checksum (GoBD, Art. 958f CO, BAO)
- Corrections only by a cancellation invoice with its own number
- Mark quotes as sent, accepted or declined; after their validity (30 days by default) they count as expired
- *Turn into invoice* makes an invoice draft from a quote
- *Duplicate* creates a copy as a draft
- History per document, for example issuing, payments, reminders, e-mails and a manual choice of tax treatment
- Lists with views (*Drafts*, *Open*, *Overdue*, *Paid*, *Cancelled*, *Written off*), filters by year and customer, totals
- Full-text search over number, customer, subject and lines
- View, download and print the PDF

Guides: [Your first invoice](/docs/getting-started/first-invoice/), [Quotes](/docs/invoices/quotes/), [Issuing and cancelling](/docs/invoices/issue-and-cancel/)

## Payments and write-offs

- Record payments with date, amount and note, also partial payments
- *Mark as paid* in one click, also for several invoices at once
- *Undo* right in the message; recorded payments can be removed again
- *Overdue* is derived from the due date every day, not stored
- Write off invoices that will not be paid, with date, reason and note; a write-off can be taken back
- A payment after the write-off is recorded as usual
- The view *Written off* shows the losses per year and currency and the tax they contain

Guides: [Payments and reminders](/docs/invoices/payments-and-reminders/), [Writing off invoices](/docs/invoices/write-off/)

## Taxes and countries

- Company seat in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Germany or Austria; customers can be anywhere
- Dated VAT rates; the rate of the service date applies, without a service date the invoice date
- Switzerland and Liechtenstein: 8.1 / 2.6 / 3.8 %, plus the earlier rates since 2011
- Germany: 19 / 7 %, plus 16 / 5 % for the second half of 2020
- Austria: 20 / 10 / 13 %
- Tax treatment decided from the company's country, the customer's country and VAT number: domestic, reverse charge, intra-Community supply, export delivery, export, small business, exempt
- Manual choice of the tax treatment with a reason that goes into the history
- Goods and services kept apart, also on mixed invoices, each with its notice
- Small businesses: invoices without VAT, with the notice the country requires (§ 19 UStG, § 6 Abs. 1 Z 27 UStG)
- Mandatory invoice details are checked before issuing
- A hint in the editor when an EU business customer lacks the VAT number for reverse charge
- VAT is calculated per rate; only the total is rounded
- Swiss 5-centime rounding to CHF 0.05, can be switched off
- Wording per country: MWST and UID in Switzerland and Liechtenstein, USt. and USt-IdNr. in Germany, USt. and UID in Austria
- More countries can be added as a country profile of their own

Guides: [Switzerland](/docs/countries/switzerland/), [Liechtenstein](/docs/countries/liechtenstein/), [Germany](/docs/countries/germany/), [Austria](/docs/countries/austria/), [More countries](/docs/countries/more-countries/)

## Payment QR codes and bank accounts

- Swiss QR-bill with payment part in CHF or EUR, for businesses in Switzerland and Liechtenstein
- QR reference with a QR-IBAN, or creditor reference (SCOR, ISO 11649) with a normal IBAN
- EPC QR code (GiroCode) on euro invoices from Germany and Austria
- EPC QR code also on euro invoices from Switzerland and Liechtenstein to customers in the EEA, when the euro account has a BIC
- Several bank accounts with IBAN, QR-IBAN, BIC and currency, one default account per currency
- Footer with all bank accounts or only the account of the invoice currency

Guides: [Switzerland](/docs/countries/switzerland/), [Germany](/docs/countries/germany/)

## E-invoices

- ZUGFeRD / Factur-X (EN 16931) embedded in the PDF of every issued invoice and cancellation invoice, for all four countries
- Every PDF is PDF/A-3b
- The embedded e-invoice can be switched off in the print template
- XRechnung 3.0 (CII) as an XML file for German public authorities
- Leitweg-ID or fixed buyer reference per customer in Germany
- Before issuing, Rhinvoice names what the e-invoice or the XRechnung still lacks
- Units as UN/ECE codes (Recommendation No. 20), as EN 16931 requires
- Checked in the automated tests with the KoSIT validator and Mustang

Guide: [E-invoices](/docs/invoices/e-invoice/)

## Customers and articles

- Customers as a company or a person, with salutation, contact person, address, e-mail, phone and group
- Customer number from its own number sequence or by hand
- Per customer: default currency, document language, payment terms, default discount, tax exemption, delivery address and internal note
- The customer's VAT number; in Germany the Leitweg-ID
- Customer page with "customer since" and all the customer's documents
- Articles with number, name, description, group, unit, VAT rate and whether they are goods or a service
- Article prices per currency (CHF and EUR)
- Archive and restore; archived entries no longer appear in selection lists
- Search by name, number, city, e-mail or description

Guide: [Customers and articles](/docs/moving/customers-and-articles/)

## Texts and print template

- Text templates for introduction, payment terms and closing, per document type, one of them as the default
- Placeholders in texts: `{nummer}`, `{datum}`, `{faellig}`, `{kunde}`, `{betrag}`, `{waehrung}`, `{firma}`
- The country's legal notices as a suggestion, with your own wording if you like
- Documents in German, English, French or Italian, chosen per document, per customer or as the company's default
- Three print templates: *Classic*, *Modern*, *Plain*
- Logo on the left or right in three sizes, font Inter or Source Serif
- Sender line above the address for the window envelope, contact details in the head, position numbers on or off
- Address field after DIN 5008 form B for window envelopes
- Preview of the print template with the latest invoice
- Issued documents keep their PDF when the print template changes

Guide: [First start](/docs/getting-started/first-start/)

## Payment reminders

- All overdue invoices with the next step under *Dunning*
- Three levels: payment reminder, 1st dunning letter, 2nd dunning letter
- Per level: days after the due date or the last deadline, new payment deadline, dunning fee
- Reminder text per level in German, English, French and Italian
- Reminder as a PDF with the open amount plus the fee
- Send reminders by e-mail
- Written-off invoices are not reminded

Guide: [Payments and reminders](/docs/invoices/payments-and-reminders/)

## Recurring invoices

- Plans every month, quarter, half-year or year
- On the date: create a draft, issue, or issue and send by e-mail
- The period covered goes onto the invoice as its service period
- Set the first and last invoice, pause and resume a plan
- Missed dates are caught up, each period only once
- Per plan a list of the invoices written, with their outcome
- *Make recurring* turns an issued invoice into a plan

Guide: [Recurring invoices](/docs/invoices/recurring/)

## Sending by e-mail

- Invoices, quotes, cancellation invoices and reminders by e-mail with their PDF
- Optionally the XRechnung as a second attachment
- Sent through your own mail server (SMTP) with STARTTLS or SSL/TLS
- Sender name, reply-to address and a copy of every e-mail to the sender
- Test e-mail under *Settings → E-mail*
- E-mail templates per document type and language, with placeholders
- Several recipients per e-mail
- Every e-mail is noted in the document's history; when the server refuses one, Rhinvoice shows its answer

Guide: [Recurring invoices](/docs/invoices/recurring/)

## Moving over from your previous program

- Customers and articles from CSV, XLSX and XLS
- Character set (UTF-8, Windows-1252) and separator are detected
- Column mapping, test run with preview and a report as CSV
- Entries that exist already: skip, update or create anew
- Save column mappings as a template
- Old invoices with their original PDFs: a list plus PDF files, a folder or ZIP files
- Invoices taken over are locked, marked *Taken over* and appear in search, revenue and the customer's history
- Open invoices taken over can be paid and written off
- The number sequence continues behind the numbers taken over

Guides: [Customers and articles](/docs/moving/customers-and-articles/), [Old invoices](/docs/moving/old-invoices/)

## Overview

- Revenue of the year against last year, with a line per month
- Open and overdue amounts per currency, by age
- The longest overdue invoices
- Open quotes with this year's acceptance rate
- Top customers of the year and expected payments per week
- Recent activity and a setup checklist

## Backup

- With Docker: a backup container of its own with restic, encrypted and deduplicated
- Daily, after issuing or cancelling, and on request with *Back up now*
- Targets: S3-compatible storage, SFTP, a folder, and through rclone Dropbox, Google Drive and others
- On Windows and Mac: backups into a folder such as OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, a network drive or a USB disk, with a recovery key
- Kept are 7 daily, 4 weekly, 12 monthly and every yearly backup
- *Settings → Backup* shows the recent backups and warns when the last successful one is older than 24 hours
- Restoring is described step by step, also onto a new computer

Guides: [Backup and restore](/docs/operations/backup/), [Backup in the program](/docs/operations/backup-desktop/), [Backup to Google Drive](/docs/operations/backup-google-drive/)

## Security

- Login with e-mail address and password; passwords are stored with argon2id
- Two-factor authentication with an authenticator app (TOTP)
- Lock after repeated failed attempts, at most 10 logins per minute and address
- Session cookie with HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, a strict Content-Security-Policy
- A new password ends the account's other sessions
- *Forgot password?*: on a server by command, on Windows and Mac with a one-time code in the data folder
- The program for Windows and Mac only answers requests to its own address (127.0.0.1 or localhost)
- Your data lies in your own database, on your computer or server

## Working with it

- Command palette with ⌘K or Ctrl+K
- Keyboard shortcuts: **I** or **N** new invoice, **Q** new quote, **C** new customer, **A** new article, **P** record a payment, **/** search, ⌘↵ save, ⌘Z undo
- **G** and a letter open a list, for example **G I** the invoices; **?** shows every shortcut
- Single-key shortcuts can be switched off under *Settings → Interaction*
- Light and dark mode, or following the system
- Interface in German (Switzerland, Germany, Austria), French, Italian and English
- Amounts and dates in the country's format, for example `CHF 1’234.50` or `1.234,50 EUR`
- Accent colour of the interface can be set, for example to the company colour
- On the phone: check the status, mark as paid, view the PDF
- Fully usable by keyboard; the automated interface tests check the pages with axe-core against WCAG 2.2 AA
- Setup wizard on the first start (country, company) and a checklist on the overview
- *About Rhinvoice* with the version and *Report a problem*

Guide: [First start](/docs/getting-started/first-start/)

## Installation and updates

- Program for Windows 10/11 with a setup, without Docker, database included (beta)
- Program for Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later (beta)
- The setups are signed; the Mac app is also notarized by Apple
- Your data stays when you uninstall
- With Docker Compose on a server, a NAS, a Raspberry Pi 4/5 or your own computer; images for `linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64`
- With Docker also several user accounts, created with `rhinvoice user create`
- A notice about a new version in the sidebar and under *About Rhinvoice*
- On Windows and Mac one click installs the update; if the new version does not start, the previous one comes back with its database
- With Docker the notice shows the commands for the update
- The update check can be switched off
- Test versions come as a separate program, *Rhinvoice Beta*, with data of its own
- Sample data for a test installation with `rhinvoice demo`

Guides: [Windows and Mac](/docs/getting-started/install-desktop/), [Installation with Docker](/docs/getting-started/installation/), [Updating](/docs/operations/update/), [Uninstalling](/docs/operations/uninstall/)
