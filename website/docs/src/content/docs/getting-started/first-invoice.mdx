You write an invoice in Rhinvoice in the editor: choose the customer, add the lines, check the **live preview** and click *Issue*. Only then does the invoice get its number and become unchangeable. After that you open the PDF, send it, and mark the invoice as paid when the money arrives.

Before your first invoice, finish the [first start](/docs/getting-started/first-start/): country, company and a bank account.

## 1. Add a customer

The editor picks customers that exist already. Add one under *Customers → New customer*, or import your customers from a CSV or Excel file ([customers and articles](/docs/moving/customers-and-articles/)).

## 2. Open the editor

Click *New invoice* in the navigation, or press **N** anywhere outside an input field. The editor shows the form on the left and the **preview** on the right, as the invoice will look on paper. On a phone or in a narrow window you switch between *Edit* and *Preview*.

## 3. Fill in the invoice

<Steps>

1. **Customer**: type a name, number or city and choose the customer. Rhinvoice shows the address as it will appear on the invoice.

2. **Dates**: leave *Date* empty to use the date of issue, and *Due date* empty to use the payment terms of the customer or company. You can also pick *Payable within* a number of days.

3. **Lines**: *Add line*, then enter description, quantity, unit and price. Articles are suggested while you type. *Apply* takes the line into the list; clicking a line in the list or in the preview opens it again. *Add text line* adds a heading or a note without amount. **Alt+↑** and **Alt+↓** move a line.

4. **Texts** (optional): intro, payment terms and closing, also from your text templates.

5. **More details** (optional): subject, reference, service period, currency and a discount for the whole document.

</Steps>

The *Tax treatment* follows from the countries of your company and the customer: domestic, reverse charge, export or small business, with the notice the law requires. Rhinvoice takes the VAT rate that applies on the service date. Details per country: [Switzerland](/docs/countries/switzerland/), [Liechtenstein](/docs/countries/liechtenstein/), [Germany](/docs/countries/germany/), [Austria](/docs/countries/austria/).

## 4. Save the draft

*Save* (**⌘↵** on a Mac, **Ctrl+Enter** on Windows and Linux) keeps the invoice as a draft. Once a draft is saved, Rhinvoice saves later changes by itself; the bar at the bottom shows *Saved* and how long ago. A draft can still be changed or deleted (*Delete draft*).

## 5. Issue the invoice

Click *Issue*. Rhinvoice asks *Issue the invoice?*: the invoice gets its final number and cannot be changed afterwards. Corrections are only possible with a cancellation invoice. Before issuing, Rhinvoice checks the mandatory details of your country and says what is missing.

<Aside type="caution" title="Issued means final">
From issuing on, content and PDF are frozen and the PDF is kept with its SHA-256 checksum. To correct an issued invoice, cancel it (*Cancel invoice* creates a cancellation invoice with its own number) and write a new one. See [Issuing and cancelling](/docs/invoices/issue-and-cancel/).
</Aside>

## 6. PDF, print, e-mail

On the issued invoice:

- *PDF* opens the PDF in a new tab, *Download* saves it, *Print* prints it.
- *Send by e-mail* sends it with the PDF attached, once the mail server is set up under *Settings → E-mail*.

The PDF carries the payment part: the Swiss QR-bill in Switzerland and Liechtenstein, the EPC QR code (GiroCode) for euro invoices in Germany and Austria. A ZUGFeRD / Factur-X e-invoice is embedded in the PDF ([E-invoices](/docs/invoices/e-invoice/)).

## 7. Mark as paid

When the money arrives, open the invoice:

- *Mark as paid* records the full amount with today's date. The message that follows has *Undo*.
- *Record payment* (key **P**) is for another date or a partial payment: enter *Payment date* and amount, then *Save payment*. The invoice then shows *Partially paid* and the open amount.

In the invoice list, *Paid* in a row marks a single invoice as paid; several invoices can be selected and marked at once. An invoice past its due date shows as *Overdue*. More in [Payments and reminders](/docs/invoices/payments-and-reminders/).

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **N** | New invoice |
| **Q** | New quote |
| **/** | Focus search |
| **⌘K** / **Ctrl+K** | Open command palette |
| **⌘↵** / **Ctrl+Enter** | Save |
| **P** | Record a payment (in a document) |
| **⌘Z** / **Ctrl+Z** | Undo the last action (while its message is visible) |
| **G** then **I** | Go to invoices |
| **?** | Show all shortcuts |

Single-key shortcuts only work while no input field is focused.
