description: Answers about Rhinvoice, the free open-source invoicing program for CH, LI, DE and AT. Cost, licence, data, GoBD, backups, e-invoices, Docker, demo.
faq:
  - q: What does Rhinvoice cost?
    a: Nothing. Rhinvoice is free and open source. There is no paid version and no hosted service, and there are no plans to turn it into a paid hosted service. You run Rhinvoice on your own server or computer.
  - q: Under which licence is Rhinvoice published?
    a: The GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0). You may use, change and share Rhinvoice freely. If you offer a changed version to others as a service, you share your changes under the same licence.
  - q: Which countries is Rhinvoice made for?
    a: For companies in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Germany and Austria, with each country's VAT rates, required details, notices and payment QR codes. Your customers can be anywhere in the world.
  - q: Will more countries be added?
    a: Rhinvoice is built for it. Everything that differs between countries lives in one country profile per country. A new country such as the Netherlands is a new profile with rates, notices, mandatory details and official sources. Requests go through the GitHub form "Tax or legal rule".
  - q: In which languages is Rhinvoice available?
    a: The interface is in German, in Swiss, German and Austrian variants, in French, Italian and English. French and Italian are machine translated; you choose the language with the globe icon. Rhinvoice writes documents in German, English, French or Italian (French and Italian as written in Switzerland).
  - q: Where are my data kept?
    a: On your own server or computer, in Rhinvoice's PostgreSQL database. It holds customers, articles, documents and the archived PDFs. Rhinvoice is not a cloud service; the data stay with you. The program for Windows and Mac keeps them in your user folder.
  - q: Does Rhinvoice meet the immutability rules of the GoBD, Art. 958f CO and the BAO?
    a: Rhinvoice implements them. From issuing on, an invoice's content and PDF are locked, directly in the database too, and the PDF is kept with its SHA-256. Corrections are made only by a cancellation invoice with its own number. Whether your bookkeeping as a whole meets the rules is something to settle with your tax adviser or fiduciary.
  - q: How does Rhinvoice back up my data?
    a: With restic, encrypted and deduplicated, daily and about two minutes after documents were issued or cancelled. Targets are S3-compatible storage, SFTP, a folder, Dropbox, Google Drive and others; the program for Windows and Mac backs up into a folder such as OneDrive or iCloud Drive. Rhinvoice keeps 7 daily, 4 weekly, 12 monthly and every yearly backup.
  - q: Can Rhinvoice create e-invoices?
    a: Yes. The PDF of every issued invoice is a PDF/A-3b and holds a ZUGFeRD / Factur-X invoice (EN 16931), unless you switch it off in the print template or required details are missing. For German public authorities Rhinvoice also writes the XRechnung 3.0. Both are checked with the KoSIT validator and Mustang.
  - q: Can I take over data from the invoicing program I used before?
    a: Yes. Rhinvoice imports customers, articles and old invoices from CSV and Excel files (XLSX, XLS), with column mapping, a test run and saved templates. Old invoices come with their original PDFs and stay unchanged.
  - q: Do I need Docker?
    a: No. On Windows 10/11 and on a Mac with Apple silicon (macOS 12 or later) Rhinvoice installs as a regular program with one setup, the database included (beta). For a server, a NAS, a Raspberry Pi or several users on different computers, it runs with Docker and Docker Compose, also on your own laptop with Docker Desktop.
  - q: Does Rhinvoice run on a Raspberry Pi or a NAS?
    a: Yes. Rhinvoice is published for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64, so also for the Raspberry Pi 4 and 5. It runs on a NAS that can run Docker with Docker Compose.
  - q: Can I use Rhinvoice on my phone?
    a: Yes. The interface adapts to the phone. On the way you check the status of invoices, mark them as paid, and open the PDF or pass it on with *Share* through the phone's share sheet.
  - q: Where do I get help, and is there a commercial licence?
    a: Questions and ideas go to the GitHub Discussions, bugs to the GitHub Issues of rhinvoice/rhinvoice. For a commercial licence, a partnership or taking over the project, write to hello@rhinvoice.com.
  - q: Can I try Rhinvoice without installing it?
    a: Yes, in the public demo for Switzerland (ch.rhinvoice.com), Germany (de.rhinvoice.com) and Austria (at.rhinvoice.com). It resets every hour and takes no uploads, so no imports, no logo and no files. It sends no e-mail.
  - q: "Why does my banking app reject the QR code from the demo?"
    a: "The demo uses official example accounts, not real ones. For Swiss invoices in CHF it is the example QR-IBAN from SIX, whose bank code belongs to no bank, so banking apps reject the QR code. The other QR codes scan, but they lead to example accounts too: please do not transfer anything. In your own installation the QR code carries your account, and your customers scan it as usual."
  - q: Can invoices be paid to another account, for example a factoring company's?
    a: "Yes. Since version 0.9.0 you can keep several bank accounts per currency and choose one per invoice in the editor. An account can be marked as a third party's, for example a financial service provider's: the invoice then prints \"Pay to:\" with the holder, and the QR-bill and e-invoice name it as payee."

Rhinvoice is a free, open-source invoicing program for small businesses in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Germany and Austria, which you run yourself with Docker. It is still in beta (version 0.x): try it on a test installation first. Here are the most frequent questions; more in [installation](/docs/getting-started/installation/), [e-invoice](/docs/invoices/e-invoice/), [backup](/docs/operations/backup/) and on the country pages for [Switzerland](/docs/countries/switzerland/), [Liechtenstein](/docs/countries/liechtenstein/), [Germany](/docs/countries/germany/) and [Austria](/docs/countries/austria/).
