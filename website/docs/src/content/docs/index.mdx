faq:
  - q: Is Rhinvoice free?
    a: Yes. Rhinvoice is free to use, change and share under the GNU Affero General Public License (AGPL). There is no paid version and no hosted service.
  - q: Which countries does Rhinvoice support?
    a: Rhinvoice is made for companies in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Germany and Austria, with the VAT rates, legal notices and payment codes of each country. Your customers can be anywhere.
  - q: Where are my data stored?
    a: On your own server or computer. Rhinvoice runs with Docker on your machine and keeps everything in its own PostgreSQL database; nothing goes to a cloud service unless you choose one as backup target.

Rhinvoice is a free, open-source invoicing program for small businesses in Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Germany and Austria. It writes quotes, invoices, cancellation invoices and payment reminders with the VAT rules, payment codes and e-invoice formats of each country, and runs with Docker on your own server or computer.
