Rhinvoice can hold **several bank accounts per currency**. One of them is the default of its currency, and every quote and invoice is paid to that account unless you **choose another one in the editor**. An account can also belong to **a third party**, for example a financial service provider that collects the payment for you: the invoice then names that third party as payee, also in the QR code and the e-invoice.

## Several accounts, one default per currency

You add accounts under *Settings → Bank accounts → Add account* with bank, account holder, IBAN, BIC and currency (see [first start](/docs/getting-started/first-start/)). Exactly one account per currency is the default: tick *Default account for this currency* on it. A new document uses the default account of its currency, for example an invoice in EUR your default EUR account.

## Choosing the account for one invoice

<Steps>

1. Open the quote or invoice in the editor.
2. In the card *Dates*, below date and due date, choose the account under *Bank details*. The field only appears when the document's currency has more than one account.
3. The live preview shows the chosen account at once, in the footer and the payment details. The invoice gets its QR code for this account when it is issued.

</Steps>

Each option shows the bank (for third-party accounts the holder) and a shortened IBAN. The default account is marked *(default)*, accounts of third parties *(third party)*. The default is preselected.

- **Changing the currency** sets the choice back to the default account of the new currency.
- **Deleting an account** never blocks: drafts that used it fall back to the default account of their currency.
- **Issued documents** keep the account they were issued with, like all their content ([issuing and cancelling](/docs/invoices/issue-and-cancel/)).
- **Recurring invoices** have the same choice: in the plan, under *More details*, next to the currency. Every invoice the plan writes is paid to that account ([recurring invoices](/docs/invoices/recurring/)).

## Accounts of a third party

Some businesses have their invoices paid to a financial service provider, for example for factoring. That account belongs to the provider, not to you, and the invoice must say so.

<Steps>

1. Under *Settings → Bank accounts*, add the provider's account with the provider as *Account holder*.
2. Tick *Account belongs to a third party (e.g. a financial service provider)*.
3. Fill in the *Address of the account holder*. Postal code and city are required.

</Steps>

The list of accounts marks it with the badge *Third-party account*. A third-party account can also be the default of its currency. Rhinvoice never guesses from the holder's name whether an account is a third party's: only the checkbox counts.

What changes on documents paid to a third-party account:

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Payment details on the PDF | "Pay to:" with the holder, IBAN and BIC (in German "Zahlung an:", in French "Paiement à :", in Italian "Pagamento a:"), also on payment reminders |
| Swiss QR-bill (CH, LI) | The holder as creditor, with the third party's address. Your own accounts keep your company's address. |
| EPC QR code (GiroCode) | The account holder as beneficiary, as with your own accounts |
| E-invoice (ZUGFeRD / Factur-X EN 16931, XRechnung) | The chosen account as payment account, and the third party as payee (BG-10, BT-59) |

<Aside type="tip" title="Most invoices go to the provider">
Make the provider's account the default of its currency, and its payment terms the default text: under *Settings → Texts*, tick *Insert into new documents (default)* on the matching *Payment terms*. New invoices then start with both. Bank accounts and payment texts are deliberately not linked: if you choose another account on one invoice, adjust the payment terms there too.
</Aside>

## Bank details in the footer

Under *Settings → Print template*, card *Footer*, *Bank details* sets which accounts the footer of your documents lists:

| *Bank details* | Footer |
|---|---|
| *Invoice currency only* (preset) | Only the account the document is paid to |
| *All accounts* | All your own accounts, for example CHF and EUR. A third party's account is added only on documents paid to it. |
| *None* | No bank details in the footer |

A third party's account never appears on documents that are not paid to it. Accounts of the same bank are listed together. The setting only changes the footer: the payment details and the QR codes always use the account the invoice is paid to. Issued documents keep their PDF and look as before.
