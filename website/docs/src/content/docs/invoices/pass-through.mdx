A **pass-through item** is an amount you paid **in the name and for the account of your customer**, such as a commercial register fee, and charge on unchanged. In Rhinvoice you choose *Pass-through item (no VAT)* as a line's *Tax rate*: the line gets no VAT, the document discount leaves it alone, and the PDF lists it in a block of its own before the total, several such lines with their sum. It only applies when the **third-party invoice is made out in your customer's name**.

<Aside type="caution" title="Only in your customer's name">
If you buy something in your own name and charge it on, it is a normal line with VAT, even when you charge it at cost. A pass-through item needs the third-party invoice addressed to your customer. The legal basis: Germany § 10 Abs. 1 Satz 4 UStG, Austria § 4 Abs. 3 UStG 1994, Switzerland and Liechtenstein Art. 24 para. 6 lit. b MWSTG, where the amount must be shown separately (Rhinvoice always does). Rhinvoice gives no tax advice: ask your fiduciary or tax adviser when in doubt.
</Aside>

## Marking a line as a pass-through item

<Steps>

1. In the editor, add the line with the description and amount of the third-party invoice, such as "Commercial register fee".
2. Under *Tax rate*, choose **Pass-through item (no VAT)**. Below it, the editor reminds you: "Only if the third-party invoice is made out in your customer's name." The choice between goods and services disappears for this line.
3. Attach the third-party invoice as a PDF (see [below](#attaching-the-third-party-invoice)).

</Steps>

An article can be a pass-through item too: choose it as the article's *Tax rate*. When you [import articles](/docs/moving/customers-and-articles/) from CSV or Excel, the tax column understands "durchlaufend", "durchlaufender Posten" and "pass-through".

## How Rhinvoice calculates them

- **No VAT.** The line is in no VAT line and adds no notice, in every tax treatment: domestic, reverse charge, export or small business. When a document has only pass-through items, the PDF shows no net total, no VAT lines and no tax notice, and reverse charge asks for no VAT number of the customer.
- **No document discount.** The discount of the document applies to the taxed lines only. An amount discount cannot be larger than those lines. When the document has only pass-through items, Rhinvoice refuses a discount.
- **In the total.** The amount counts in full in the total, so in the Swiss QR-bill, the EPC QR code, payments, open amounts and reminders.
- **Rounding at the end.** The Swiss 5-centime rounding applies to the final total only.

## Example

A fiduciary charges 4 hours of work at CHF 120.00 and the fee of the commercial register office, CHF 250.35, paid in the customer's name. 5-centime rounding is switched on.

| | CHF |
|---|---|
| Work, 4 h × 120.00 | 480.00 |
| MWST 8.1 % on 480.00 | 38.88 |
| *Pass-through items:* commercial register fee | 250.35 |
| Rounding | 0.02 |
| **Total** | **769.25** |

The QR-bill asks for CHF 769.25. VAT is only due on CHF 480.00.

## On the PDF

Pass-through items are not in the table of positions. They follow the VAT lines, before the rounding and the total, under the title **"Pass-through items (paid in the name and for the account of the customer)"**, each with its description and amount. In French it reads "Débours (payés au nom et pour le compte du client)", in Italian "Spese anticipate (pagate in nome e per conto del cliente)", in German "Durchlaufende Posten (im Namen und auf Rechnung des Kunden bezahlt)". This is the separate statement the Swiss VAT Act asks for. Documents without pass-through items look exactly as before.

## Attaching the third-party invoice

Your customer usually needs the third-party invoice with yours. Every draft, invoice or quote, has a folded section **Attachments** below *More details*:

<Steps>

1. Open *Attachments* and click **Add PDF**. You can choose several files at once.
2. In a new document, the files wait until you save it for the first time. If you leave it without saving, nothing is kept. <Since version="0.11.0" />
3. Open a file to check it, or remove it, as long as the document is a draft.

</Steps>

A draft holds at most **10 PDFs of up to 20 MB together**, so that they fit into one e-mail; Rhinvoice refuses a PDF beyond that when you upload it, and other file types too. When the draft has a file of the same name already, the new one gets a number, such as `Notar (2).pdf`. When you [issue](/docs/invoices/issue-and-cancel/) the document, its attachments are frozen with it, like the PDF: they are never changed or removed again (GoBD, Art. 958f CO). Issued documents show them in a card *Attachments*. The document's own PDF stays as it is; the attachments are separate files. Deleting a draft deletes its attachments; duplicates and cancellation invoices do not copy them. The public demo takes no uploads.

## Sending by e-mail

*Send by e-mail* attaches the document's PDF, then the XRechnung if you choose it, then the attachments. The mail dialog lists them all. A document's attachments are at most 20 MB together, because many mail servers refuse larger mails; Rhinvoice checks this when you upload them.

## Dashboard, export and e-invoice

- **Dashboard:** pass-through items are not revenue. The revenue chart, the year to date and the best customers leave them out; open and overdue amounts include them, since your customer owes them.
- **[Export for bookkeeping](/docs/invoices/export/):** a column *Pass-through items* between *Tax* and *Gross*, also in the totals, when the period has such items. *Net* and *Tax* leave them out, *Gross* includes them. With the ZIP, each document's attachments follow its PDF, named after the number and their own name, such as `R20260042_Handelsregister.pdf`. Rhinvoice shortens long names to 200 characters.
- **[E-invoice](/docs/invoices/e-invoice/):** pass-through lines are exempt, category E, with the exemption reason code VATEX-EU-79-C and a short reason text, in a VAT breakdown of their own. When the document has other exempt lines, they share that breakdown, with both reasons as text and no code. For small businesses in Switzerland and Liechtenstein without a VAT or tax number they are in category O, like the other lines. Rhinvoice's samples pass the official KoSIT validator and Mustang.
