A quote in Rhinvoice goes from *Draft* to *Sent* and then to *Accepted*, *Declined* or *Expired*. A sent or accepted quote becomes an invoice draft with **Turn into invoice**, without entering anything twice.

## Writing a quote

<Steps>

1. Open *Quotes → New quote*.
2. Choose the customer and enter the lines. The preview on the right shows the quote as it will be printed.
3. Under *Valid until*, enter how long the quote is valid. Left empty, it is 30 days from the date.
4. Click **Mark as sent**. The quote gets its number and is locked from then on.

</Steps>

A draft has no number yet. You can change it at any time, or remove it with *Delete draft* in the *More actions* menu (the button with three dots). The number follows the quote sequence under *Settings → Number sequences*; the preset pattern is `A{YYYY}{NNNN}`, for example A20260001.

<Aside type="note" title="Sent means locked">
From *Sent* on, a quote does not change any more. For a changed version choose *More actions → Duplicate*: the copy is a new draft with the same content.
</Aside>

## The states of a quote

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *Draft* | Being written, no number yet |
| *Sent* | Number assigned, content locked |
| *Accepted* | The customer said yes |
| *Declined* | The customer said no |
| *Expired* | Sent, but the date under *Valid until* has passed |

You set *Accepted* and *Declined* yourself with the buttons of the same name on the sent quote. Nobody sets *Expired*: Rhinvoice derives it from the validity date. In the list under *Quotes*, *View* filters by each of these states.

## Turning a quote into an invoice

On a sent or accepted quote, choose *More actions → Turn into invoice*. Rhinvoice creates an **invoice draft**:

- with the same lines, discounts and currency as the quote,
- with the texts you wrote yourself; where the quote still has the standard texts for quotes, the draft gets the standard texts for invoices,
- dated today, like a new invoice; the due date follows when it is issued,
- with a link to the quote. A sent quote counts as *Accepted* from then on.

Check the draft, complete it if needed and issue it like any other invoice ([issuing and cancelling](/docs/invoices/issue-and-cancel/)). The VAT rate then follows the invoice's date of supply, not the quote's.

A quote becomes an invoice only **once**. If you delete the invoice draft, the quote is free again. *Open invoice* or *Open invoice draft* takes you from the quote to the invoice, *Open source document* back.

## PDF and sending

On the quote, **PDF** opens the printed version in a new tab, **Download** saves it and **Print** prints it right away. When sending by e-mail is set up, **Send by e-mail** sends a sent quote with its PDF to the customer. The mail is then noted in the quote's *History*.
